TruCoach Legal & Compliance Center

Applicable Entity: TruCoach Platform (India)

Effective Date: August 17, 2026

Governing Laws: Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, Information Technology Act 2000, & RBI Guidelines for Payment Aggregators.

1. PRIVACY POLICY

1.1 Data We Collect

  • User & Coach Profiles: Full name, mobile phone number, email address, physical address/pincode, academy affiliation, and coaching certifications.
  • Athletes & Minors: Athlete name, age, Parent/Guardian details. All data regarding Students is collected strictly with the explicit digital consent of their verified parent or legal guardian or Academy Coach.
  • Payments & Billing: Gateway order IDs, transaction timestamps, payment status, and bank reference/UTR numbers. (Note: Card numbers and UPI PINs are processed directly by RBI-licensed payment gateways and TruCoach does not collect or process biometric data; FaceID/fingerprint auth is handled entirely by the device OS).
  • Location & Geolocation: Precise GPS location and pincodes used exclusively for discovering nearby sports academies, batches, and tournaments.
  • Device Security & Biometrics: FaceID and fingerprint authentication are processed locally within the device's secure hardware enclave and are never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

1.2 Purpose & Use of Data

Collected data is used strictly to:

  • Manage academy batches, rosters, and schedules.
  • Calculate live attendance and generate monthly progress report cards.
  • Route automated billing links and instant digital receipts via payment gateways.
  • Display nearby sports coaching facilities on the community discovery page.

1.3 Strict Data Protection & Non-Disclosure

  • Zero Commercial Sale: TruCoach has not signed, and will never sign, any agreement to sell, rent, monetize, or share student, parent, or coach data with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing agencies.
  • No Commercial Behavioural Ads: The platform does not serve commercial third-party ads. Minor profiles are strictly private and shielded from public web crawlers.
  • Authorized Infrastructure Sub-processors: Data is processed solely through enterprise compliance partners:
    • Cashfree Payments: For processing secure UPI/card payment sessions and payouts.
    • Meta (WhatsApp Cloud API): For server-side delivery of transactional attendance, billing notices, and official payment receipts.
    • Supabase / Cloud Infrastructure: Encrypted database storage with Row-Level Security (RLS).

1.4 Meta WhatsApp Communication & Opt-Out

By registering their phone number on TruCoach, users explicitly consent to receiving operational and transactional WhatsApp messages (attendance absentee alerts, emergency / Personal cancellations, schedule shifts, and payment receipts). Users may manage notification preferences or request communication opt-out at any time via App Settings or by emailing support@trucoach.co.in

1.5 Data Retention & User Rights (DPDP Act 2023)

Users have the absolute right to review, update, export, or request permanent deletion of their account and associated student records. Deletion requests can be initiated from the App profile screen or by contacting TruCoach at support@trucoach.co.in.

2. TERMS & CONDITIONS

2.1 Role & Platform Intermediary Status

TruCoach operates exclusively as a digital Automation platfrom connecting sports academies, certified / Experienced coaches, and parents. TruCoach does not operate sports physical facilities and is not an employer of independent academy coaches.

2.2 Academy & Coach Verification Gate

All new Academy Admins and Coaches must undergo verification review before creating batches or issuing fee invoices. Uploading fabricated certifications or misrepresenting credentials results in immediate account termination.

2.3 SaaS Subscription & Free Trial

  • 15-Day Academy Free Trial: New academies receive 15 days of full platform access with zero software licensing charges.
  • Subscription Billing: Continued operational use after 15 days (tracking attendance, issuing fee invoices, automated broadcasts) transitions the academy account to the applicable SaaS tier.
  • Unbilled Usage: Termination of service does not waive outstanding SaaS subscription fees incurred during active billing cycles.

2.4 Payments & Zero-Custody Escrow Model

  • Direct P2B Settlement: Tuition fees paid by parents are settled directly to the designated academy/coach merchant account via Payment Gateway(eg. Cashfree). TruCoach maintains a zero-custody model and does not hold or pool student tuition funds in escrow.
  • GST & Tax Compliance: Academies are solely responsible for calculating and remitting applicable Goods and Services Tax (GST) on coaching fees. Standard 18% GST applies to SaaS software subscription invoices issued by TruCoach.

2.5 Code of Conduct & Content Standards

  • Zero Tolerance for Inappropriate Content: Coaches, academies, and users must not strictly upload obscene, defamatory, sexually suggestive, or abusive text, media, or comments.
  • Mandatory Venue & Schedule Updates: Academies must immediately update the app if a physical venue relocates or closes. TruCoach is not liable for disputes arising from unannounced physical facility closures.

2.6 Physical Sports & Injury Liability Waiver

Athletic training inherently involves physical exertion and bodily injury risk. Parents and guardians acknowledge this risk upon enrolling students. TruCoach, as a software intermediary, bears no liability for injuries, accidents, equipment failures, or medical emergencies occurring on physical sports grounds.

Property Damage Liability: Any physical damages caused by a student to the academy's premises, facilities, or training equipment—whether during a free trial or active enrollment—must be fully compensated by the student's parent or legal guardian. TruCoach assumes no responsibility or liability for property damage occurring at physical training centers.

Code of Conduct & Misconduct Policy: TruCoach mandates strict professionalism, discipline, and respect between tutors, coaches, academies, parents, and students. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy for any form of abuse or misconduct. Any coach, tutor, or academy reported for misconduct toward students will be permanently banned from the platform. The respective coach, tutor, or academy is solely and legally responsible for addressing parents, legal guardians, or legal authorities regarding any allegations of misconduct. TruCoach acts only as a software intermediary and holds no liability for the behavioral misconduct of independent parties.

3. CANCELLATION & REFUND POLICY

3.1 Academy SaaS Platform Subscriptions

  • Academies may cancel their SaaS software subscription at any time via the Admin Command Center.
  • Subscriptions cancelled within 48 hours of initial activation without substantial usage are eligible for No Charges
  • Once an active billing cycle has processed automated student billing links, software subscription fees for that cycle are non-refundable.

3.2 Student Academy Tuition Fees

  • TruCoach provides the software conduit for fee invoicing. The underlying tuition agreement is between the Parent and the respective Sports Academy.
  • Refund Eligibility: Tuition fee refund requests (e.g., student relocation, medical leaves, voluntary withdrawal) are governed directly by the respective Academy’s refund rules.
  • If an academy approves a tuition refund, it can be initiated through the payment dashboard, and the refunded amount will credit back to the parent's original payment source (UPI/Bank/Card) within 5–7 business days as per banking standards.

3.3 Free Trial Cancellation

Parents may cancel scheduled 1-day Free Trial sessions at any time prior to session start with zero penalty. The free trial credit is immediately restored to the parent's account.

4. DIGITAL SERVICE DELIVERY POLICY

  • Instant Fulfillment: All software features, attendance dashboards, Community listings, and digital receipts are fulfilled immediately and electronically upon successful account creation or payment confirmation.
  • No physical merchandise shipping is involved unless explicitly approved by Platfrom Administrators (TruCoach)
